SEC parser call-site fix v8

Root cause confirmed from Render logs:
- The XML normalization helper existed, but parse_form4(), parse_144(), and the XML ownership path still called ET.fromstring(raw) directly.
- This caused repeated 'XML or text declaration not at start of entity' and 'mismatched tag' failures.

Fixes:
1. Form 4 now always passes through _parse_sec_xml().
2. Form 144 now always passes through _parse_sec_xml().
3. XML 13D/13G ownership parsing now passes through _parse_sec_xml().
4. Atom feed parsing is intentionally unchanged.
5. Repetitive parse errors are sampled instead of logged for every filing; the final aggregate scan summary remains.
